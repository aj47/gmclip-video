# S4 — gmclip text raster vs libass (B-10)

**Verdict: GO.** gmclip's cue raster (parley shaping + vello_cpu at the scalar fallback level,
`crates/gmclip-render/src/text/raster.rs`) matches libass on every distinct golden event:
627/627 displayed events at SSIM ≥ 0.98 (minimum 0.9920). `em_per_size(Neometric Heavy)` = 0.8332 is confirmed,
and line advance equals `size`. The bundled OFL face for CI goldens is **Figtree Black**.

## Method

- References: `tools/s4_compare.py` renders each distinct golden event with ffmpeg's `ass`
  filter. Each event is a one-event script with the golden file's own header and styles,
  shown at t = 0 on a 1080×1920 frame.
  - The binary is `static_ffmpeg` (evermeet build N-105504-g04cc7a5548, 2022). RT burned its
    captions with this build, so its libass is the reference.
  - vf_ass writes no alpha, so each event is rendered over black and over white and
    un-composited: α = 1 − (W − B)/255, premultiplied colour = B.
- Ours: the lab test `crates/gmclip-render/tests/s4_raster.rs` writes our tile for each event.
  Run it with `GMCLIP_S4_OUT=DIR cargo test --release -p gmclip-render --features lab --test s4_raster`.
- Distinct events: 628 distinct (style, Dialogue text) pairs among the 649 Dialogue lines of the
  11 golden ASS files. One, h79's pill "GENERATING 0:30" (32.87–32.90 s), shows on no frame
  under either time rule and is not imported, so 627 are compared.
- References are kept as compressed crops (the reference's own coverage + 48 px, with its
  canvas origin) of the transparent 1080×1920 render, which is equivalent to the full frame;
  they are scratch, regenerated by the tool, and not committed.
- SSIM: Wang et al., 11-tap Gaussian (σ 1.5), on BT.709 luma.
  - The region is the union of both images' coverage, padded by 8 px.
  - Both images are composited over three backgrounds (RT's `#0c1118`, mid grey, white); an
    event's score is the minimum of the three.
  - Also recorded: the bounding-box offset of α ≥ 128 (ref − ours) and the mean |Δα|.
- Per-event results: `spikes/S4/ssim.tsv`, including bbox and alpha-centroid offsets.
- How coarse the gate is: libass against itself shifted by 1/8 px scores about 0.996, by
  1/4 px 0.985, by 1/2 px 0.95. So 0.98 alone would pass a systematic quarter-pixel error;
  the centroid columns bound ours at ≤ 0.21 px (means ≤ 0.07 px), and the bounding boxes of
  α ≥ 128 agree within 1 px. H-06's golden frames should gate on an offset too, not SSIM only.

Alpha-centroid offsets (ours − libass): Speech mean (+0.007, −0.059) px, max |·| (0.21,
0.11); Hook mean (+0.04, +0.07); Pill mean (0.00, −0.04).

627/627 events reach SSIM ≥ 0.98, so no failures are listed. The worst are "could do it."
(h48) and "come on." (h88), both captions at 0.9920. The residual is not mainly the outline:
with outline and shadow off the worst captions still score 0.993–0.996 (reviewer
measurement). It is sub-pixel placement and edge anti-aliasing of the glyphs themselves —
libass positions bitmaps through a quantised transform and rasterises its own way, and our
glyphs sit up to ~0.1 px off vertically (snapping the baseline down to 1/8 px instead of to
the nearest made it worse: min 0.9896).

### What it took (each step measured on the same set)

1. Raw parley layout reached min 0.63: libass text came out 1–12 px wider. Two causes:
   - **libass shapes without `kern`.** An ASS script gets kerning only with `Kerning: yes`, and
     no RT script sets it.
   - **libass advances are hinted at 256 px.** It shapes at a FreeType REAL_DIM size of 256 px
     using hinted metrics (`FT_LOAD_DEFAULT`, `ass_shaper.c`), so each advance is the
     whole-pixel advance at 256 (`FT_PIX_ROUND(FT_MulFix(adv, x_scale))`), then scaled by
     `size/256` and rounded to 1/64. That is about +0.1 px per glyph against the exact
     advance.

   With both fixed, captions and hooks reach ≥ 0.992.
2. Pills reached min 0.955.
   - **libass turns off `liga`/`clig` whenever Spacing ≠ 0.** Without this, "fi" became a
     ligature.
   - **BorderStyle 3 boxes are drawn per glyph, merged per colour run, and composited run by
     run.** Where two runs' translucent boxes overlap (2 × 16 px), the box is darker. RT's
     shipped pills show this band at every colour change. We draw one box per (line, run), each
     as its own fill.

   With both fixed, the pill minimum is 0.9972.
3. Glyph origins snap to 1/8 px and lines are centred on their summed 26.6 advances, as in
   libass `align_lines` and `get_base_point`.

## Cost

All 627 distinct golden events rasterise in 2.7–3.7 s in a release build on the M1 Pro,
single-threaded, tile writes included (≈ 4–6 ms per cue; a reviewer measured 10–15 ms for a
long outlined caption on a loaded machine).

## Determinism (B-09)

- `crates/gmclip-render/tests/text_raster_hashes.rs` renders 50 fixtures with Figtree Black.
  The fixtures cover all 9 alignments, two-line text, colour runs, kerning on/off, accents,
  ligatures, outline + hard shadow, a blurred translucent shadow, BS3 and rounded boxes, and
  negative letter spacing. Each tile's FNV-1a 64 is compared with
  `tests/data/text_raster_figtree.tsv`.
- Identical on Apple M1 Pro arm64 in debug and release, and on `x86_64-apple-darwin` under
  Rosetta.
- **Not yet run on x86 Linux.** The Docker daemon is not running on this Mac, and A-03 runs it
  on hosted CI.
- Why it should hold elsewhere:
  - The geometry uses only + − × ÷ √: flattening, a dilation whose arcs use a fixed rotation
    constant, and a blur kernel built from a polynomial exp.
  - Points are quantised to 1/256 px before rasterising.
  - vello_cpu is pinned to `Level::fallback()`, single-threaded, u8 pipeline.

## Findings for other tickets

1. **RT's frames are libass on ffmpeg's truncated milliseconds** (decision 0003).
   - ffmpeg's `ass` filter passes libass `(int64)(pts · (1/30) · 1000)` computed in doubles.
     That lands 1 ms short on 51 of the first 2000 frames (111, 207, 222, 237, 399, …), so an
     event that starts or ends exactly there switches one frame later than `⌈0.3·cs⌉`.
   - Checked on `h79-v4.mp4`: "let's also try" (13.30 s) first appears on frame 400, not 399.
   - The 11 golden files have 20 such Dialogue edges.
   - `text::ass::display_frame` implements decision 0003's `F(cs)` and is the one copy in the
     workspace: the importer uses it and the converter's `rounding::ass_frame` calls it.
2. **SPEC §5.10 describes a later `story_reel.py` than the one that made the goldens.** It
   says the cue hold is "never past … a jump" and that cues "clamp to the enclosing continuous
   stretch". Those are AJ's uncommitted working-tree edits; the goldens match the committed
   revision byte for byte (`crates/gmclip-render/captions/story_v5.md`).
3. **SPEC §7 says "outline (`stroke_glyphs` …)".** We build the round-join outline as an exact
   dilation instead of vello's stroker. kurbo's stroker uses trigonometry from the platform
   libm, which would break byte identity. The shadow follows §7's A8 mask and our own
   fixed-point Gaussian when blur > 0; with blur 0 it draws the offset geometry directly, which
   gives the same result.
