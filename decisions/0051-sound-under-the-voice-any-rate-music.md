# 0051 — Sound: a recording's system audio under the mic, any sample rate, music with ducking

Date: 2026-10-26 · Decided by: agent (the sound lane of AJ's five-agent pass) · Status: adopted, AJ may veto

## Context
Each part played one audio source (decision 0009's `audio_track` names one track per source), so a gmclip recording's system audio — the first screen file's audio track (decision 0045) — could not play under the face file's mic. The model's `AudioOverlay` (music, `duck_under_speech`) had no command, plan field or mix. Only 48 kHz AAC played. Playback's mix must stay bit-identical to the export's (`playback.rs`; R-COMP-1's sound half).

## Decision
- **RenderPlan `/1` adds** (`schemas/renderplan@1.json`; a plan using none of them is written `/0` by `schema_for`, so every existing plan, golden and `render_sig` is byte-identical):
  - `parts[].audio.under: [{source, first_sample, gain_db}]` (1–8; gain −60…+12 dB): other sources heard under the part's own audio, each from its `first_sample` for the part's length. Order of rule 7 becomes *part gain (the part's own audio) → + under sources at their gains → part fades → sum → envelopes → loudness*. Not on a sped part (`Invalid`). Rule 7's continuity stays the part's own audio's; at a continuous join an under source that carries on (same source, adjacent samples) at another gain ramps linearly in amplitude over 10 ms (480 samples) at the start of the later part, one the next part drops ramps out over the part's last 10 ms and one that starts there ramps in from silence.
  - `audio.overlays: [{id, source, first_sample, out_samples, gain_db, fade_in_samples?, fade_out_samples?, duck?: {depth_db, attack_samples, release_samples}}]`: summed with the parts before the envelopes; output sample `n` reads `first_sample + n − out_samples[0]` (silence past the file's end), × gain × linear fades × the duck.
  - `audio.speech: [range]` (in order, apart): where the cut's voice speaks. A ducked overlay is lowered by `depth_db × w(n)`, `w` = 1 inside a range, rising linearly over `attack_samples` before it and falling over `release_samples` after it (the larger where they meet).
- **One mix path.** `AudioPlan::mix` is `mix_range` over the whole cut, and playback reads sources through the export's own `PlanPcm` (every source the plan hears; playback reads one it cannot open as silence), so the playback mix and the export mix cannot drift apart. The decoder takes its rate and channel count from the AudioSpecificConfig (AudioToolbox writes `channelcount 2` in the sample entry of mono AAC).
- **Any sample rate, and PCM.** A heard source at another rate than 48 kHz is converted at decode (`gmclip-media::audio::resample`: Blackman-windowed sinc, 32 taps a side at the source rate, widened going down, cut off at 0.95 × the lower Nyquist, one normalised tap set per phase, exact phases up to 4096 of them; each output sample a fixed function of its index, so chunks equal the whole); `first_sample` counts samples of that 48 kHz stream. Under `/0` an `expect.audio.rate` ≠ 48 000 stays `Unsupported` (a renderer that predates this refuses such plans, rule 12); the planner writes `/1` when a heard source has another rate. Linear PCM is read as stored — MOV `lpcm`/`sowt`/`twos`/`in24`/`in32`/`fl32`/`fl64` through the MP4 demuxer's PCM packets, and **WAV** (a new `Container::Wav`: integer or float `fmt `, `WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE`) — 16/24/32-bit integer or 32/64-bit float, mono or stereo; integers scale by `2^(bits−1)`. No codec is involved, so no OS decoder: the same bytes give the same samples everywhere. 8-bit PCM (its signedness differs by container) and more than two channels are refused. The import sheet now says AAC at any rate is "transcribed and exported (converted to 48 kHz)" and PCM "exported, but not transcribed" (transcripts still read AAC).
- **Model.** `SyncMember.under_db` (the member's sound under the group's audio; `None` or ≤ −60 dB is not heard) and `Part.under_db` (in place of every member's level on that part). The planner hears, on each part of a sync group that does not change speed, every other member with an audio track, at its offset (member time = group time − offset, in whole samples from the part's own `first_sample`).
- **Default.** `open_recording` (decision 0045) sets the screen member to **−12 dB**: audible under the voice, below it. Other pairings (an OBS hi-res picture beside its voice file, whose sound usually duplicates the voice) stay unheard, so their plans are unchanged.
- **Music and sound effects.** The model's `AudioOverlay` plans as `audio.overlays[]`: its anchors give `out_samples` (cut at the cut's end, and at the end of a source stretch); a stored asset (`asset_add` now takes `audio/mp4` and `audio/wav`, up to 128 MB) is a plan source `asset-<id>` read from its start, a span of a source or group reads that media's sound from `src_in`; fades in ms (at most half the sound each); `duck_under_speech {depth_db, attack_ms, release_ms}` is lowered by `|depth_db|`. When a sound ducks, the planner writes `audio.speech`: every word of each part's active transcript that the part plays, mapped through the part's clock (speed and ramps included), gaps under 300 ms closed. Without a transcript there is nothing to duck under and the sound plays at its gain. Commands: `audio_overlay_add {cut, media, start, end, gain_db? (−18), fade_in_ms? (500), fade_out_ms? (1000), duck?}`, `audio_overlay_set {overlay, start?, end?, gain_db?, fade_in_ms?, fade_out_ms?, duck? | no_duck}`, `audio_overlay_delete`. The app's `music_add {cut, path}` stores an M4A or WAV and lays it over the whole cut ducked 12 dB (200 ms attack, 600 ms release); Inspector → Sound and camera lists the sounds (gain, duck, remove). Agents reach every overlay command; storing a new file is the host's, as for images.
- **Commands** (registry, so agents and the UI share them): `sync_group_create` members take `under_db`; `sync_under_set {member, under_db}`; `part_set_under {parts, under_db, span?}` — a span of one part (source times) splits it at the span's edges in the same step and changes only the middle. Inspector → Sound and camera: "System audio" dB per part and "Use on every part".

## Evidence
- `gmclip-plan/tests/v1.rs::sound_under_overlays_and_speech`: all three parse and round-trip under `/1`, each is refused under `/0`, and every reference, range, order and the sped-part rule fires.
- `gmclip-media` `audio::graph::tests::sources_under_a_part_sum_at_their_gain_and_ramp_at_joins` (exact samples: voice +6 dB plus the under source at −12 dB; halfway through the −12 → −6 dB ramp; the ramp out; chunked mix = whole mix) and `overlays_fade_and_duck_under_speech` (fade in from silence, full, ducked −12 dB inside speech, half depth halfway through the attack, the release, fade out; chunked = whole).
- `gmclip-core/tests/sound.rs`: a recording's two parts plan `/1` with the screen file under the voice at −12 dB on the same samples; per-part −20 and −60 (dropped), `sync_under_set`, a span split into three with the voice continuous across it, one undo step; without `under_db` the plan is byte-identical to before (`/0`); a sped part hears only its voice.
- `gmclip-app` `playback::tests::the_playback_mix_is_the_export_mix_for_two_sources` (macOS, real AAC through AudioToolbox): the playback mixer's 1024-frame chunks equal the export's whole mix bit for bit for two parts with a mono voice, a stereo source under it at −12/−6 dB and a ducked overlay; removing the under source and overlay changes the mix by more than 0.03 RMS.
- `gmclip-media` `backend::aac::tests::the_config_says_the_rate_and_channels`.
- `gmclip-core/tests/sound.rs::music_plays_over_the_cut_and_ducks_under_the_voice`: an M4A asset over the two parts plans at −18 dB, fades 24 000/48 000 samples, duck −12 dB with 9 600/28 800-sample attack and release, speech `[[24 000, 67 200], [240 000, 264 000]]` (two words 0.1 s apart are one stretch; a word outside the parts is not heard); `no_duck` drops the speech; a one-second stretch of the screen file's sound is cut to one second; delete and undo; out-of-range gain or depth, duck with `no_duck`, and a PNG are refused.
- `gmclip-app` `tests/sound.rs::music_over_the_reel` (macOS, h79 from the lab media): `music_add` stores a 44.1 kHz M4A, the cut plans `/1` with the asset at its stored path, and the export's mix through AudioToolbox carries the music at 0.0267 RMS (a 0.3 tone at −18 dB); `audio_overlay_set` and `audio_overlay_delete` show in the panel's view.
- `gmclip-media` `audio::resample::tests`: a 1 kHz tone from 44.1, 32, 22.05, 96 kHz and an odd 44 056 Hz is the 48 kHz tone within 5·10⁻⁴ (−60 dB of 0.5); a 30 kHz tone at 96 kHz leaks under 1 %; uneven stereo chunks equal the whole read bit for bit. `backend::pcm::tests` (packets, skip, lead, the end; 16/24-bit both byte orders, 32/64-bit float), `demux::wav::tests` (packets of 1024 frames, float, a truncated data chunk, an unsupported tag).
- `gmclip-app` `playback::tests::sound_at_any_rate_and_pcm_plays_as_the_export_mixes_it` (macOS): a 44.1 kHz AAC voice (220 Hz → 440 ± 2 sign changes a second at 48 kHz, RMS 0.19–0.23 of a 0.3 tone) and a 22.05 kHz 24-bit WAV under it (1 kHz → 2000 ± 10); the plan validates under `/1` and is refused under `/0`; playback's chunks equal the export's mix bit for bit. `gmclip-core/tests/planner.rs::a_refusal_says_what_about_the_video_does_not_fit`: 44.1 kHz sound now plans `/1`.
