# 0033 — RenderPlan `/1`: speed ramps (`parts[].speed.ramp`) and pinned covers (`layers[].track`); a track across takes

Date: 2026-10-17 · Decided by: agent (lane K7, AJ's recommendations of 2026-10-16: the K3.4/K3.5 leftovers) · Status: adopted, AJ may veto

## Context
Lane K3 left three things open: a speed that eases inside a part (0030: "split it meanwhile"), covers that follow motion (0031: "covers are not pinned yet"), and a track that keeps following past the take it started in (0031: "one take per track"). A ramp and a moving cover change pixels and samples, so each is a plan field (rule 12); the track across takes is model, planner and app work.

## Decision
- **`parts[].speed.ramp {to, from, frames, curve}`** (`$defs/speed_ramp`): the speed eases from the part's `num/den` (A) to `to` (B, ¼–4, ≠ A) over the part's output frames `[from, from + frames)` along one of the four ease curves, then holds B. With a ramp A may be 1. Terms ≤ 10⁴, `frames` 1–10⁶, inside the part.
  - **Picture**: the source the part has advanced by output frame `k` is the speed's exact integral, `advance(k) = A·k + (B − A)·T/(12N²)`, `T = 12N³∫curve` — an integer cubic for each curve, so i128 rationals, no float (`PartSpeed::advance`). Output frame `k` shows source time `(first_frame + advance(k)) / output.fps` by decision 0017's rule (`retime::source_time`, `nominal_frame`, `picture_frame`).
  - **Sound**: the same integral on the sample clock (`advance_f64`); WSOLA follows it (`stretch::stretch_by`, a constant map is bit-identical to `stretch`). The part reads `⌈advance_f64(n/spf)·spf⌉` source samples, stretched whole once per plan (chunked mix = whole mix). Muted unless both A and B are ½×–2×. Never a continuous join.
- **`layers[].track {x, y, keys}`** (`$defs/layer_track`), covers only: on output frame `f` the place moves by `p(f) − (x, y)` rounded to whole pixels (`p` the keys' interpolation, held past the ends, as event tracks) and the crop by that move × crop/place. Drawn in `frame_draw`, so R-COMP-1 holds by construction.
- **Model (additive, epoch 3)**: `Part.ramp {to, src_start, src_end, curve}` on the source clock, clipped to the part (`clock::speed_profile`; a ramp wholly before `src_in` leaves the part at `to`); the clock maps local ↔ source through it (`part_duration`, `local_to_src`, `src_to_local`, `src_to_cut`), and captions, comments, tap marks and review frames read it there. A split inside a ramp gives each side its piece, meeting at the speed there (exact for linear; the eases are re-applied per piece). `Layer.track` (an `OverlayTrack`) on cover layers.
- **Planner**: a ramp starts `r0/A` frames in and lasts its source over its average speed `A + (B − A)∫curve` (the one tie rule); the part runs at B until the plan's integral reaches the part's source. A pinned cover's track per plan part: the point at the part's first and last frames (interpolated on the source clock) plus every key the part plays, through the cover's own crop and place, held so the crop stays on the picture. Overlay tracks now map every key through every part that plays it, in output order (a reordered or repeated take keeps its keys).
- **Commands**: `part_set_ramp {part, ramp: {to, start_ms, dur_ms, curve} | null}` (registry, M2; `part_set_speed` replaces a ramp); `layer_track_set {layer, track | null}` (registry, M2; covers only). App: `overlay_track` follows every take of the video the overlay spans (`track::track_spans`: a take ≤ 5 s after the last is reached through the frames between, any other restarts Vision where the point was); `cover_track {cut, layer, frame}` pins a cover from the playhead through every take it is on; `cover_add` ("Blur") adds a blur cover at a canvas point on the take's stack (its layout, else the framing's).
- **Synced groups stay refused** ("synced cameras are not followed yet"), in the error and in both Pin to motion hints.
- Plans with neither field are unchanged (`schema_for`): every existing plan, golden and `render_sig` is byte-identical.
