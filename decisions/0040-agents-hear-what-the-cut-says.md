# 0040 — Agents hear what the cut says: a script read, a retakes sweep, lint on speech

Date: 2026-10-18 · Decided by: agent (AJ: "fix it all completely at a systems thinking level", after "a lot of spaces and some sentences dont make sense and also a lot of repeats still. just seems very sloppy") · Status: adopted

## Context
AJ's session `s1792388405606-0` ("arrange this project and remove dead space and repeat takes") left a 707 s cut with 33 s of silence around one sentence, three takes of "All the Git repositories…", and parts beginning "a button for me…" or "can see I have…". The trace showed why. The agent edited blind: `transcript_read` shows the source, struck words and all, and `cut_get` only source times, so three strikes failed ("no part of this cut plays them") and it fell back to `part_trim` / `part_split` at times it computed, landing mid-sentence. A strike listing words with gaps took the words between too (`words_strike` cuts first → last). It never used `sweep_pauses`. Retakes (SPEC §5.5) had no sweep. Its check was `verify{joins}`, which needs an export and failed with "the export's job id", so it ran `lint`, which hears nothing of speech, and reported done. The session file kept 600 bytes of each tool input, too little to see which words it struck.

## Decision
- **`gmclip_core::script`: what a cut says.** The words each part plays (midpoint inside the part) from the media's active transcript, in cut order on the cut's clock; silence before, after and between a part's words (≥ 1 s); a part that starts or ends mid-sentence (the source sentence goes on and the cut does not follow it; a strike inside one sentence is not flagged); an edge inside a word; parts with no speech. One reading feeds the agent read, lint and the sweep, so they agree.
- **Retakes (SPEC §5.5, now M2).** Over the cut's kept words in order, within 20 s and 40 words: an earlier attempt that opens the same way (the same word, "I'm"/"I" alike, or another little opener before the same two words) and is said again word for word (a single letter twice is a spelling, kept), nearly (≥ 0.7 of its words in order, ≥ 0.8 for a whole sentence, or a whole sentence with the same three opening words and half its content words), or broken off and begun again (≤ 12 words, unfinished; its second word comes again, half its words after a pause, or ≤ 4 words broken off on "gonna", "the", "to"…). Similar and restarted attempts begin where an attempt can (cut start, sentence end, pause, part edge, struck word) unless they share their opening words. The longest valid attempt goes; the restart may sit up to three words later where they match better; struck words are not compared again, so of three sign-offs the last stays. The span runs from the attempt's first word to where the next kept word starts in that part.
- **`sweep_retakes{cut, dry_run?}`** (holder command and registry entry beside `sweep_pauses` / `sweep_fillers`; `sweep_preview{kind: retakes}`; `SweepKind::Retakes`): previews each earlier and later attempt, applies as one `sweep_apply` recording the transcripts' revisions. The app's Tighten tab offers **Retakes**.
- **`cut_get{projection: words}`**: each part's words with their ids (untrusted), its notes ("6.2 s silent before", "starts mid-sentence", "says nothing for 12.0 s"), the retakes left and a one-line summary, paged.
- **Lint hears speech** (warnings, with transcripts): `retake`, `dead_air`, `mid_sentence`, `cuts_word`, `silent_part` (a part that says nothing in a cut that talks). Messages carry times and counts, never transcript text.
- **`words_strike`** refuses a list of more than two words with a hole ("every word of one span … or just its first and last"); two words stay the span's ends.
- **`verify`** without an export says to export first and to check the edit with the words read and lint; its tool doc says so.
- **The server instructions** give every agent the loop: read the words, `sweep_retakes` / `sweep_fillers` / `sweep_pauses` (preview, apply), strike other words (never typed part times), read again until nothing starts or ends mid-sentence and no retake is left, `lint` before done; B-roll as a video overlay over speech, not silent parts between sentences. The launcher's edit pass says the same.
- **Session files** keep tool inputs up to 64 KiB and results up to 16 KiB (the UI shows the first 4000 characters).
- **ASR word times are loose.** Parakeet folds the silence beside a word into it ("BB." 6.64–10.64 s; "Continue" timed from long before it is said): a part plays a word when it holds the largest share of it, at least 0.12 s or half the word (`gmclip_core::script::plays_in`, shared by the words read, lint, the sweeps' sliver and quiet-edge checks and the window's word strip, so a played word is never shown struck); a word is spoken for at most 0.8 s, so the silence after it counts as dead air; an edge cuts a word only in a word timed ≤ 0.6 s, 0.08 s inside on both sides. A forward jump inside one recording within 20 s (a struck retake or pause) is not a dangling sentence.
- **Sweeps leave no slivers.** After a sweep's spans, a part of the swept media under 0.5 s that says nothing goes too (the live run's pause sweep after the retakes sweep left 23 parts of 0.13 s).
- **Found by the live re-run, fixed:** the retakes sweep recorded the transcripts' revisions in full and `sweep_apply` refused them (> 300 bytes): it records their fingerprint (`retakes@1/<n>t/<hash>`). One soundless clip in the cut failed `sweep_pauses` / `sweep_fillers` outright ("the source has no audio"): such a source's parts count as `parts_without_audio` and the rest is swept. A strike that reaches a part's first or last word takes the part's wordless head or tail with it (the window's strikes did; the core's now do, so agents leave no slivers of silence). A part whose video has no audio track is a silent part (lint `silent_part`, "no sound" in the words read). "Undo its edits" after the app restarted failed twice: the project was not open (a window action naming a library project now opens it from the library folder; the session's undo does too), and the run was only in memory (`run_undo` reads a run persisted with the project).
