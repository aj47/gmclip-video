# 0028 — RenderPlan `/1`: the backdrop and clip events (SPEC §5.11)

Date: 2026-10-17 · Decided by: agent (lane K3, AJ's pick of 2026-10-16 "backdrop and clips layer") · Status: adopted, AJ may veto

## Context
SPEC §5.11 [M4] has a backdrop (`background: backdrop{asset, loop, fit}`) that "runs on the `out` clock, continuous across joins", and the model has carried `Background::Backdrop` and `OverlayKind::Video` since M1 — but RenderPlan could say neither, so the planner refused a backdrop and nothing could place a video over a span. Lane K4's B-roll put another project's moment in as a still frame "because no clip layer exists". Both change pixels, so they need plan fields (rule 12).

## Decision
- **`gmclip.renderplan/1` adds** (`schemas/renderplan@1.json`):
  - `background {kind: backdrop, rgb, crop, place, video?: clip_picture, image?: id}` — exactly one of `video` and `image` (an `images[]` id); `crop` in picture px is drawn into `place` in canvas px (aspect as rule 5); `rgb` shows where it does not cover.
  - `clip_events[] {id, out, layer?, picture: clip_picture, crop, place, opacity?}` — a video picture-in-picture on `[out[0], out[1])`, **silent**.
  - `$defs/clip_picture {source, first_frame, fps?, frames?, loop?}`: the media's `k`-th output frame is `k mod frames` when it loops, else `min(k, frames − 1)` (no `frames`: `k`); then presentation index `first_frame + k'`, or with `fps` the frame at or before `(first_frame + k') / output.fps` on the source's own clock (decision 0017's rule, `retime::clip_frame`). For the backdrop `k` is the output frame itself, so it never restarts at a part join; for a clip event it counts from `out[0]`.
- **Drawing**: both enter `frame_draw`, the one draw list the preview and the export share (R-COMP-1 by construction). A backdrop video is the first layer (`@backdrop`), clip events are layers after every part layer (`@clip:<id>`), by `layer` then array order, under every image, shape and text event; they go through the ordinary layer path (crop, Lanczos, place, opacity) on CPU, GPU and the Windows pipeline alike. A still backdrop is an image tile (crop resampled to its place by exact area weights) drawn right over the clear. `@` ids cannot collide with plan ids (`^[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]+$`); the viewer's gizmos skip them. The backdrop and the clip events read **decode streams of their own** (`@backdrop`, `@clip:<source>`), so a part and a clip of one recording never seek one decoder back and forth.
- **Model (additive, epoch 3)**: `Asset.probe {w, h, fps?, frames?}`; `Overlay.video {media: AssetOrSpan, loop, crop?}`. `asset_add` takes `video/mp4` and `video/quicktime` (with their probe; still ≤ 20 MB) and records a probe on an existing asset that had none. `framing_set` takes a backdrop (a stored video, or a PNG with its size; `cover` or `contain` — `stretch` is refused, a picture keeps its aspect). `overlay_add {kind: video}` takes a span of a project video or a video asset.
- **Planner**: a video asset becomes a plan source (`asset-<ulid>`, its probe as `expect`, one source when the backdrop and a clip share a file); its length in output frames is `⌊frames · out / src⌋`; `fps` is written when the rates differ. A span's first frame is its nearest output frame on the source clock. A plan with neither is unchanged (`schema_for`), so every existing plan, golden and `render_sig` is byte-identical.
- **App**: `backdrop_set {cut, path|asset, loop, fit}` and `video_overlay_add {cut, frame, path|asset|span, loop?, dur_ms?}`. A picked MP4/MOV ≤ 20 MB is stored as it is, by its hash (`<sha>.mp4|.mov` beside the PNGs); anything longer or in another container is cut to an excerpt by the exporter (no ffmpeg): 10 s ≤ 1080 px for a backdrop, 15 s ≤ 960 px for a clip. The Inspector's "Images and callouts" panel gets a path field, **Backdrop** (with Loop, Fill/Fit and remove) and **Video**. **B-roll** offers **As a clip** on every suggestion: this project's video as a span overlay, another project's as an excerpt stored with the cut, silent, picture-in-picture over the words.

## Limits
- A clip event is silent; its audio is not mixed (a later field can add it).
- The model's `Clip` entity (intro/outro/after-part slots, SPEC §5.11 first sentence) is not planned yet; this decision covers the backdrop and picture-in-picture video overlays only.
