# 0046 — Screen Studio controls: sections, cursor feel, screen look, camera bubble, one-click look

Date: 2026-10-19 · Decided by: AJ ("can we also have screen studio like controls for it in the editor in post"; all five; "drag sections like screen studio on top of parts"; motion blur "default off if costly") · Status: adopted

## Context
Decision 0045 records screen and face with a cursor log and redraws the cursor; zoom to focus writes eased crop splits per part. Screen Studio's editor is what AJ wants on top: zoom sections dragged on a lane above the clips, a cursor that feels smooth, a screen that floats on a background with rounded corners and a shadow, a camera bubble, layouts that switch over time, and one click that makes a raw recording look finished. gmclip has backgrounds (plates, backdrops, blurred source, decision 0029), layouts per part, eased splits (0016) and the recorded cursor (0045); sections, the cursor's feel, layer frames and the preset are new.

## Decision
- **Sections** (`Section`, a new cut-level entity, additive): `{parent: Cut, framing?: Framing (absent: every framing), start: Anchor, end: Anchor, kind}`, drawn as lanes **above the parts** on the timeline, dragged freely across part edges like Screen Studio's. Times are cut times anchored as overlays are, so trims and reorders carry them as they carry overlays.
  - `kind: zoom {depth 1–6 (2), target: cursor | focus | point {x, y} (normalised in the target layer's crop), ease_in_ms (700), ease_out_ms (700), layer: screen | cursor | all (screen), camera_scale? (0.3–1: camera layers shrink toward their corner while zoomed), motion_blur (false), auto (false)}`. The target layer is drawn through a crop `depth`× tighter than its own, centred on the target and kept inside its own crop, eased in and out with ease-in-out. `cursor` follows the recorded cursor (0045) through a critically damped spring, so the crop glides; `focus` aims at the caret or the focused field; `point` holds still. A source without a log can use `point` only.
  - `kind: layout {layout: Layout, ease_ms (400)}`: over its span the framing's layers take the layout, eased from and back to what was there (the 0016 ease), e.g. camera full, screen only, screen + camera.
  - Commands: `section_add {cut, framing?, start_ms, end_ms, kind}`, `section_set {section, start_ms?, end_ms?, kind?}`, `section_remove {section}`, `section_list {cut}` (cut-ms start/end, kind), all undoable; `zoom_auto {cut, framing?, max_zoom?, dry_run?}` turns the input log into zoom sections (0045's zoom-to-focus rules) and replaces the earlier `auto` ones, leaving hand-made sections alone.
  - RenderPlan `/1`: `zooms[] {id, out: [f0, f1), layers: [layer ids by part], depth, target: {cursor: <cursors[].id>} | {focus: <cursors[].id>} | {point: [x, y]}, ease_in, ease_out (frames), camera_scale?, camera_layers?, motion_blur?}`; layout sections plan as eased splits of the parts they cover (no new field). The compositor applies `zooms` in `frame_draw`, so preview and export match (R-COMP-1).
- **Cursor feel** (`SourceCursor`, additive fields; RenderPlan `cursors[]` mirrors them): `smoothness` 0–1 (0 = the raw path; a critically damped spring whose time constant grows to 250 ms at 1; replaces `smooth_ms` in the UI, which stays readable), `click: none | ring | ripple` (from `clicks`), `click_shrink` (the cursor dips to 80 % for 120 ms on a click), `shake_px` (moves smaller than this, while slow, are held still; 0 off), `loop_ms` (the last N ms glide back to the first position, for loops; 0 off), `motion_blur` (false).
- **Screen look**: `Layer.frame? {radius (fraction of the layer's short side, 0–0.5; 0.5 + square = circle), shadow? {blur, offset_y, opacity, colour}, border? {width, colour}}` (model and RenderPlan layer field, `/1`); padding is the layer's place (as now). More built-in plates (Screen Studio-like gradients) in the style library.
- **Camera bubble**: a camera layer with a circle or rounded-square frame and a border, in a corner, sized from 10–40 % of the short side; built-in layouts "Screen + camera bubble" (four corners), "Camera full", "Screen only" to use in layout sections.
- **One-click look**: `screen_studio_apply {cut, framing?}` on a recording: padding 6 %, radius 0.03, a soft shadow, a built-in gradient plate, smoothness 0.6, ripple clicks, the camera as a bottom-right bubble, and `zoom_auto`; one undo step. Also a style-library template.
- **Motion blur is off by default** (zoom and cursor): it renders several sub-frames per frame. When on: 5 sub-samples across a 180° shutter, only on frames where the zoom or cursor moves.

## Evidence
To come with each part's tests.
- Cursor feel: `SourceCursor` gains `click` (`none | ring | ripple`; absent reads `clicks`, true = ring), `smoothness` (0–1), `click_shrink`, `shake_px` (0–20), `loop_ms` (0–10 000), `motion_blur` (false), each skipped when off so projects and plans without them keep their bytes and `render_sig`; `source_cursor_set` takes the same names (a shown `click` keeps `clicks` true; `clicks` alone clears `click`). RenderPlan `/1` `cursors[]` mirrors them (`click` only for a ripple, `shake_px` a number), clamped by the planner. `gmclip-plan/tests/v1.rs::cursor_feel` (absent: no new keys; parses, round-trips, ranges refused), `gmclip-core/tests/recorded_cursor.rs::the_cursor_feel_plans_through_clamped`, `gmclip-core/tests/source_cursor.rs` (set, ranges refused, undone).
- The compositor (`compositor/cursor.rs`): shake hold, then a critically damped spring (τ = 250 ms × smoothness, exact steps of 4 ms simulated from 12 τ before the drawn time on a grid ending at it), then the loop's ease-in-out glide over each part's last `loop_ms` to its first frame's position. Every position is a pure function of the source time (the hold replays from the last sample where history cannot matter: a gap, off the picture, or 40 px in 100 ms), so no state is carried between frames. Motion blur sums 5 copies across half a frame into one tile at 1/5 each (solid where they overlap) when the cursor moved over 2 canvas px. `gmclip-render/tests/recorded_cursor.rs`: `the_spring_is_the_same_seeked_or_played_through` (55 frames, every feel on: one layer front to back ≡ a fresh layer per frame back to front, byte for byte; the spring lags a sweep and rests on the raw path), `smoothness_0_is_the_raw_path`, `shake_is_held_still` (±1.5 px tremble held at `shake_px` 4; a sweep still moves), `a_loop_ends_where_the_part_began`, `a_ripple_fills_and_a_click_shrinks_the_cursor` (a ring's centre is clear, a ripple's ≈ 20 %; 32 px → 26 px 60 ms after the click, back after 120 ms), `motion_blur_is_off_unless_asked_for` (absent ≡ false: one cursor-sized tile; on, smeared back along the move only when it moves over 2 px a frame). The Metal `the_shared_compose_draws_the_cursor` still passes.
- Sections (model, commands): `Section {cut, framing?, start, end, kind}` (`gmclip-model` kind `section`, `MODEL_EPOCH` 6, additive), `SectionKind::Zoom(ZoomSpec)` with the listed defaults and `Layout {layout, ease_ms 400}`. `section_add`/`section_set`/`section_remove`/`section_list` take and answer cut ms; a time is stored as the app's dragged overlay stores it (`lanes::overlay_time`): the cut's edges as cut-edge anchors, else a raw source-clock anchor on the part that plays it (an end on the part that plays the ms before it). Moving an automatic zoom without a new kind makes it hand-made. `zoom_auto` is answered by the holder (`gmclip-mcp` `cursor::zoom_auto`, the input log through `zoom::zoom_path` at 10 Hz per part: caret/field keys → `focus`, click keys → `cursor`, depth = layer crop / key crop, an idle key ends it after 700 ms) and writes through `section_auto_apply`, which replaces only the automatic zooms of that framing in one transaction. `gmclip-core/tests/sections.rs`: `a_zoom_section_is_added_listed_in_cut_ms_and_undone` (1200–4800 ms across two part edges round-trips exactly; 0 and past the end are cut edges; undo/redo), `a_section_moves_with_its_media_when_a_part_is_trimmed`, `section_set_moves_an_edge_changes_the_kind_and_makes_an_auto_zoom_hand_made`, `bad_sections_are_refused_at_their_field`, `zoom_auto_replaces_only_the_earlier_automatic_zooms_of_its_framing`; `gmclip-model/tests/entities.rs` sample; `gmclip-mcp/tests/media_tools.rs::zoom_auto_writes_zoom_sections_from_the_input_log` (focus 500–1500 ms on the caret, cursor 1500–2000 ms on the click; applied twice: 2 replaced, the hand-made zoom kept).
- Sections (plan, compositor): RenderPlan `/1` `zooms[] {id, out, layers: [{part, layer}], depth, target: {cursor}|{focus}|{point: [x, y]}, ease_in, ease_out, camera_scale?, camera_layers?, motion_blur?}` (`schemas/renderplan@1.json` `$defs/zoom`, `layer_ref`; validation: ids, `out` inside, layers of overlapping parts and never covers, a named cursor, eases inside the span, depth 1–6). The planner (`planner/sections.rs`) reads each anchor as an overlay's, names per part the screen/detail, cursor or all layers (else the bottom one; never a cut-out), the first named layer's cursor for a cursor/focus target (none: the centre), the `cam` layers for `camera_scale`, and shortens eases that do not fit in proportion. `gmclip-plan/tests/v1.rs::zoom_sections`; `gmclip-core/tests/sections.rs::a_cut_without_sections_plans_as_before` (`/0`, byte for byte after a section is added and removed), `a_zoom_section_plans_its_frames_layers_and_camera` (1.2–4.8 s → frames 36–144, 700/500 ms → 21/15 frames, another framing's zoom left out, a 400 ms zoom's eases shortened to 6+6), `a_zoom_on_a_recording_follows_its_recorded_cursor`. The compositor (`compositor/zoom.rs`) applies them in `FrameComposer::compose` before the cursor tiles: ease-in-out progress, the target through a critically damped spring (τ 250 ms, the closed form one output frame at a time from the zoom's first frame, cached per zoom), the crop `depth`× tighter inside the layer's own, a zoom starting where another ends on the same layer easing from its last crop, cameras shrunk toward their nearest corner. `gmclip-render/tests/zoom_sections.rs`: `the_crop_eases_in_and_out_around_its_target` (progress 0, ½, 1 exact), `a_cursor_target_springs_the_same_seeked_or_played`, `the_recorded_cursor_follows_the_zoomed_crop`, `camera_layers_shrink_toward_their_corner`, `a_zoom_that_starts_where_another_ends_carries_on_from_its_crop`, `the_shared_compose_draws_the_zoom` (Metal, `render_frames`).
- Not yet: layout sections are stored, listed and validated but not planned (the eased splits of the parts they cover); a zoom's `motion_blur` is carried in the plan but not drawn; a `cursor`/`focus` zoom on a source whose cursor is hidden holds on the centre (the plan has no `cursors[]` entry for a hidden cursor).
- Screen look (model, commands): `Layer.frame? {radius, shadow? {blur, offset_y, opacity, colour}, border? {width, colour}}` (additive, skipped when absent; `LayerPatch.frame` set/clear per part, `LayerPreset.frame` in saved layouts). Units: `radius` a fraction of the placed layer's short side (0–0.5); shadow `blur` (0–0.5), `offset_y` (−0.5–0.5) and border `width` (0–0.1) fractions of the canvas's short side, so a preview at display size draws the same frame scaled; colours `#RRGGBB`. `layer_frame_set {layer, frame|null, part?, framing?}` (on the layer, or one part's override; never on a cover). RenderPlan `/1` `layers[].frame {radius, shadow? {blur, offset_y, opacity, rgb}, border? {width, rgb}}` (`$defs/layer_frame`; validation: not on a cover); `schema_for` is `/1` with any. `gmclip-plan/tests/v1.rs::layer_frames` (round trip under `/1`, refused under `/0`, ranges, a cover); `gmclip-core/tests/screen_look.rs::a_layer_frame_is_set_cleared_and_planned` (every part's cam framed, one part's override cleared, bad values refused at their field, undone: `/0` and the frameless plan's bytes exactly).
- Screen look (compositor): the shape is the place with corners of radius `r`; `d` is the signed distance from a pixel centre to its edge. The picture's alpha is `clamp(½ − d)` in place of the rectangle's coverage (equal to it along straight edges), × opacity, × the matte where there is one; the shadow (the shape moved down, `smoothstep(clamp(½ − d′/blur))` × opacity × the layer's opacity) is drawn just before the layer and the border (`clamp(½ − d) − clamp(½ − d − width)`) just after it, so frames keep their place among layers and the image events between them (0042). CPU `cpu::draw_frame`; GPU: `shape_cov` in the layer passes and one `FRAME_SHADER` pass each for shadow and border (`PARAM_SIZE` 112 → 128). `gmclip-render/tests/layer_frames.rs` (CPU: corners cut and edges kept, radius 0.5 on a square covers π r² within 0.5 %, the shadow full under and half at its moved edge and gone past blur/2, a 4 px border inside the arc, opacity scaling all three, a plan frame reaching the draw list); `gmclip-render/tests/gpu_compose.rs::gpu_frames_match_the_cpu` (Metal, M1 Pro: a padded screen with a shadow and border, a circle camera bubble with a white border over it, a 0.8-opacity detail with a hard shadow, a tile among them: max |GPU − CPU| 1 code Y and CbCr on both the smooth and the noise picture, gate 2; the existing layers/covers/tiles cases unchanged at 1). Not measured: a framed export through `render_frames` (the preview and the export compose on this one GPU path).
- Screen look (plates): six Screen Studio-like built-in plates (vertical gradients): Dusk, Ocean, Mint, Sunset, Graphite, Lilac, listed with the four of 0029 (`gmclip-core/tests/screen_look.rs::the_screen_studio_plates_are_built_in`).
- Camera bubble: the camera of a 0045 recording is the face file, another member of the part's sync group, so a layer whose `member` is another member now plans as a live picture of that file: RenderPlan `/1` `layers[].first_frame` with `picture_source` (frame `first_frame + (f − out[0])`, `freeze_first` its first frame; validation: only with another source, not held, not on a sped or rate-converting part; the export decodes it as its own stream, as a hold's). `gmclip-plan/tests/v1.rs::another_source_read_live`; `gmclip-render/tests/layer_frames.rs::another_source_is_read_live_from_its_first_frame`.
- Camera bubble (layouts): `layout_builtin_list {cut?}` → `{layouts: [{name, needs_screen, needs_camera, available?}]}` and `layout_builtin_add {cut, framing, name, parts?, size? (0.1–0.4, 0.22), shape? (circle|rounded)}` → `{id, layers: [{id, of: screen|camera}]}`, one undo step, for "Screen + camera bubble (bottom right | bottom left | top right | top left)", "Camera full" and "Screen only". The screen: a source with `cursor`, else the group's picture member, else a single source's screen layer crop or stream-layout region; padded 6 % of the short side, radius 0.03 and a soft shadow. The camera: the paired face file (another group member with a picture) or a single source's `cam` layer crop or region, squared about its centre, 22 % of the short side, 4 % from the corner, a circle with a 0.005 white border and a soft shadow, above the screen; "Camera full" crops it to the canvas. `gmclip-core/tests/screen_look.rs::builtin_layouts_from_a_recordings_screen_and_face` (a face + 16:10 screen pair at 1920×1080: the exact places, crops, frames and member; the part linked; planned `/1` with the camera reading the face file live from frame 300; the plan parses; camera full, screen only; three undos remove them), `builtin_layouts_from_one_sources_regions` (a cam region squared to 432 px; a source without one offers only "Screen only").

- One-click look: `screen_studio_apply` is an app command (`app/src-tauri/src/screenstudio.rs`): a restore point first (returned as `before`; "Undo look" is one `restore_to`, since human edits are never grouped in a run), then the plate, the built-in bubble layout (screen only without a camera), the cursor feel (smoothness 0.6, ripple, shrink) and `zoom_auto` through the holder's planner reading the input track from the app's own asset folder. `app/src-tauri/tests/recording.rs::the_screen_studio_look_plans_from_a_recording` (lab: the screen render `renders/own-vm-1080.mp4` in a synthetic session with a click at 2 s and typing into a field from 3.5 s): the plan has the plate, a framed screen layer, the cursor's smoothness, a cursor zoom at the click and a focus zoom on the caret; restoring `before` removes them. Stills at frames 10, 66 and 140 show the padded rounded screen on the gradient, the ease into the click zoom and the 2× zoom on the caret.
- Layout sections (planner): a layout section plans as eased splits of the parts it covers (`planner/sections.rs`: `layout_spans`, `layout_at`, `inner_edges`, `map_layout`). Its anchors are read as a zoom section's, before the part loop (the parts' output lengths ahead, `part_out_lens`), and the section in force at a frame is the latest-starting one covering it. A plan part is split wherever the section in force changes (a section's start and end) as at a cut visual split, never inside a part at another speed (it takes what is in force at its start); `plan_part_owners` counts the same splits, so the app's and holder's plan-part → model-part maps stay right. Over a section the part's layers take the layout's values by role (each layout picture layer gives the first part layer of its role its crop, place, z, opacity, visibility and frame; the part's layer keeps its source, time map, matte, mirror and feather; a part layer the layout does not map keeps its own, eased splits included). At an edge the layers it maps ease (ease-in-out, `ease_ms` of the section starting, else of the one ending) from what was drawn to where they settle: `ease.from_*` is the plan part before's eased value at that frame inside one model part (so adjacent sections ease straight from one layout to the next, and a section starting mid-ease starts from there), or, at a model part's first frame mid-ease, the part's own states before and after at the ease's progress over what is left of it. A layer the layout hides or shows fades (drawn at opacity 0 where hidden). No split at an ease's end: a `layers[].ease` holds once done. `gmclip-core/tests/sections.rs`: `a_layout_section_takes_its_stack_inside_eases_at_its_edges_and_leaves_the_rest` (1–3 s on 3 × 2 s parts → plan parts 0–30, 30–60, 60–90, 90–120, 120–180; owners follow; outside layers and pictures equal the plan without it; 12-frame eases in and out; the hidden screen fades out and back), `an_ease_crossing_a_part_edge_carries_on_from_where_it_was` (a section 6 frames before a part edge: the next part's ease starts halfway, 6 frames left, where the one before ends), `adjacent_layout_sections_ease_from_one_layout_to_the_next` (camera full → screen half: from the first layout, the camera the second does not map back to its own), `a_cut_without_sections_plans_as_before` (a layout section added and removed: the `/0` bytes again). The other gmclip-core suites pass unchanged.
- Zoom motion blur (compositor): with `motion_blur: true`, on a frame whose drawn crop moved since the frame before (a crop corner over 2 canvas px, the cursor's `BLUR_MIN_PX`), the zoomed layer is drawn as the mean of 5 sub-samples across the last half frame (180° shutter, as the cursor's): sample `j` the crop at `f − j/8` on the straight line to the frame before's. `ZoomLayer::apply` notes them; `ZoomLayer::expand_blur` turns the layer into 5 copies in the draw list, the `k`-th at `1/k` of its opacity (the running mean: exact for an opaque layer; a translucent one or a rounded frame's anti-aliased edge shows the background a little less), the shadow under the first only, and moves `tiles_at` and the cursor tiles (`remap_tiles`) to follow. It runs in `FrameComposer::compose` after the cursor tiles, so the GPU path (preview and export) and the CPU reference draw the same list: no compositor change and no divergence. Its first frame, still frames and blur off draw exactly as before. `gmclip-render/tests/zoom_sections.rs`: `motion_blur_draws_only_the_frames_where_the_zoom_moves` (CPU at ¼ canvas: off, the draw is `apply`'s alone; frames 0, 30, 55 identical draws and pixels with it on; easing frame 3: 5 copies at 1, ½, ⅓, ¼, ⅕, the last half a frame back, pixels inside the layer's place equal the mean of the 5 sub-samples drawn alone within 1e-3, nothing outside it changed), `tiles_among_the_layers_follow_the_copies`, `the_shared_compose_draws_the_motion_blur` (Metal, `render_frames` on a testsrc picture: frames 0 and 8 byte-identical with and without, frame 3 differs). The cost is 4 more layer draws on a moving frame only.
