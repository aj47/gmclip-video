# 0052 — Recordings: apps left as they were, markers in the edit, a 9:16 screen over camera

Date: 2026-10-26 · Decided by: agent (task from AJ: "fix its side effect and make what it records useful in the edit") · Status: adopted, AJ may veto

## Context
Decision 0045's focus probe sets `AXManualAccessibility` on every app it sees and `AXEnhancedUserInterface` on Chrome-family browsers, and nothing turned them off: after a recording, Chrome stayed in screen-reader mode (slower, and it behaves differently) until it was restarted. The markers AJ drops with ⌃⌥M while recording went into the input track and nothing read them. And a 9:16 of a recording showed only a crop of the screen: the presets crop one picture, and a recording's face is a file of its own.

## Decision
- **Accessibility switches are undone (§5.18).** The probe turns a switch on only when the app does not already report it on, and notes each one it turned on (pid, bundle id, attribute). Dropping the probe turns them off again, so a stop, a cancel or a dropped recorder all restore them. An app that quit, or a pid that is another app by then, is left alone. Nothing is turned off while VoiceOver runs (it wants them on). Chromium and Electron answer an error for sets that took effect, so the set's answer is not trusted and every switch tried is undone. While recording, the list is kept in the session folder as `accessibility.json`. A stop removes it. Opening a session a crash left (`complete: false`) turns its switches off and removes the file.
- **Markers in the edit (§5.18).** `Source.markers` (additive, skipped when empty): the marker times on that file's clock, in the order pressed. Opening a recording stores the session's markers on every file of it (screens and face) with `source_markers_set {source, markers_ms}` (undoable). `marker_list {cut}` answers `{markers: [{n, cut_ms, part, source, src_ms}]}` by cut time. A marker is listed once per part that plays it: a part of a sync group reads it through its members' segment offsets, at the part's speed, and the two files of one recording give it once. `cut_get` (summary and parts) carries the first 20 / 100 as `markers: [{n, cut_s, part}]` with `markers_total`. The timeline draws each as a red tick with a dot in the ruler; hovering it shows "Marker n · m:ss.s". Recordings opened before this have no markers until opened again.
- **"Screen over camera" (§5.7).** A seventh built-in layout (`layout_builtin_list`/`layout_builtin_add`, so agents reach it): the screen across the full width at the top, whole (cut top and bottom only past 55 % of the height, a portrait display), and the camera below it filling the rest, cropped about its centre, edge to edge without frames. The first 9:16 framing of a cut links every part whose sync group has a screen and a separate camera file to it, one layout per group. Other parts, and cuts of one video, get the presets as before.
