1. Open the long video

Download and open gmclip, create a project and drop in the recording: MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM. If you stream, add the chat log too. gmclip transcribes the video on your machine and times every word, so you can read an hour-long stream in minutes.

2. Find the moments

Three ways, and you can mix them:

  • Read. Skim the transcript or search it by words or by meaning.
  • Mark. Tap to mark a moment while you watch. Chat-log spikes from a stream show up as moments on their own.
  • Ask your agent. If you use Claude Code, Codex, pi or opencode, ask it to find the moments, then sweep for missed ones. It runs on your own plan and only when you click. See editing with Claude Code.

3. Cut each Short by editing words

Each moment becomes its own cut. Strike the setup you don’t need and start on the line that hooks. Tighten removes pauses and filler words in one step. Shorts work best between 20 and 60 seconds, so cut until every sentence earns its place.

4. Reframe to 9:16

Every cut keeps a 9:16 and a 16:9 framing. For a talking head, use full screen and let follow the action keep you centred. For screen recordings, use the screen-plus-facecam preset so the content and your face both fit. Blurred fill covers the gaps when a shot doesn’t fill the frame.

5. Add captions and a hook

Most people watch Shorts with the sound off at first. Burn in 1–3 word captions with keywords in yellow, and add a text hook for the first seconds. Save your look as a caption style and every new cut gets it.

6. Check on your phone, then export

Scan the QR code to watch each cut on your phone and keep, change or drop it. Export with the Reels preset (it suits Shorts and TikTok too) at −16 LUFS loudness so every clip sounds the same. gmclip drafts a title from the hook and copy for each platform.

Make the long version from the same edit

Because every cut keeps 16:9 as well, the same project gives you a tightened YouTube video with chapters. One recording in, every cut out.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. gmclip is open source under MIT or Apache-2.0, with no watermark, credits or account.

How long can the video be?

gmclip is built for long recordings such as multi-hour streams. Transcription time depends on your computer.

Does it post to YouTube or TikTok?

Not yet. It exports files and drafts the copy; you upload them.
