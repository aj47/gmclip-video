Where gmclip fits

Screen Studio shines when the recording is the video: a two-minute product walkthrough where every click zooms in. gmclip is for when the recording is raw material. You open a one-hour tutorial or stream, read it as text, strike everything that isn’t worth keeping, and cut several Shorts and a tighter YouTube video from the same take.

  • Screen-plus-facecam layouts for 9:16, so the code or slides and your face both fit
  • Follow-the-action crop and punch-ins on stressed words
  • Tighten removes pauses and filler words in one step
  • Several recordings in one project, including a separate voice track paired with the picture
  • Your own agent finds the moments worth posting and drafts the cuts

On Windows

Screen Studio has no Windows version and says none is planned. gmclip runs on Windows 10 (1809) and 11. Record with OBS or the Windows Snipping Tool, open the file in gmclip, and edit it the same way as on a Mac. Recording straight into a project is Mac only for now.

When Screen Studio is the better pick

  • You make short product demos and want automatic zoom on every click with no editing.
  • You want cursor smoothing, motion blur and polished backgrounds out of the box.
  • You want share links straight from the app.

Plenty of people use both: Screen Studio for a polished demo, gmclip to turn a long session into clips.

Questions

Is there a free Screen Studio alternative?

gmclip is free and open source with no watermark. For recording alone on any platform, OBS Studio is free and open source too, and its files open in gmclip.

Does gmclip zoom on clicks automatically?

No. It has a crop that follows the action and punch-ins on stressed words, but not click-driven zoom.
