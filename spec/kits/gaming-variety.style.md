# Style rules: gaming / variety

- Open on the action or the reaction, never on a menu, a loading screen or a greeting.
- Keep the camera and the game together in one frame. Never cut the face off.
- Captions sit in the seam between the camera and the game, 1 to 3 words at a time.
- Leave chat and alert overlays out of the frame unless the moment is about them.
- 8 to 45 seconds. End on the reaction.
