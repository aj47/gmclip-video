# Rubric: talking head / podcast (v1)

Score each candidate moment 2–5 by whether it is a complete thought with a point: a claim
and its reason, a story and its turn, or a question and its answer.

- **5**: a strong, specific claim or a story with a turn, complete in under a minute, that
  a stranger would argue with or share.
- **4**: a clear point with one example, complete on its own.
- **3**: interesting, but it leans on what was said before.
- **2**: small talk, a thought that trails off, or an answer without its question.

Span: start on the sentence that makes the point (or the question it answers), not the
lead-up; end when the thought lands. 20–90 seconds for 9:16.

Types: `opinion`, `story`, `tip`, `reaction`, `result`.
