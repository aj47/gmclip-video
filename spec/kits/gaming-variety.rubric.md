# Rubric: gaming / variety (v1)

Score each candidate moment 2–5 by how fast a stranger who has never seen the stream gets
it and wants to see the end.

- **5**: a clutch, a fail or a reaction that lands in the first three seconds with no
  context: the play and the streamer's reaction are both in the clip, and it ends on the
  reaction.
- **4**: a clear moment that needs one line of context, which the clip itself carries
  (the streamer says what is at stake just before).
- **3**: funny or impressive for regulars, but a stranger needs the stream's history.
- **2**: chat banter, menus, loading, or a moment whose payoff is off screen.

Span: start on the beat right before the play (the setup a stranger needs, at most a few
seconds), end one breath after the reaction. 8–45 seconds for 9:16.

Types: `reaction`, `clutch`, `fail`, `chat`, `story`, `tip`.

Trigger phrases ("clip that", "let's go", "no way", "did you see that"…) are hints of
where to look, never moments by themselves: read around each one and judge the moment.
