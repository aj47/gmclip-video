How a long video becomes Shorts in gmclip

  1. Open the recording. Drop in an MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM: a stream, a podcast, a talk. gmclip transcribes it on your machine, word by word.
  2. Find the moments. Ask your agent (Claude Code, Codex, pi or opencode) to find them, then to sweep for any it missed. Spikes in a stream’s chat log become moments too, and you can tap to mark one while you watch. Nothing runs until you click.
  3. Cut by editing words. Each moment becomes a cut. Strike the sentences you don’t want, and Tighten removes pauses and filler words.
  4. Frame and caption. Pick full screen or screen-plus-facecam, let the crop follow the action, and burn in 1–3 word captions with keywords in yellow. Add a text hook at the top.
  5. Export. Reels and YouTube presets at 1080p, 1440p or 4K, at −16 LUFS loudness, with a title drafted from the hook.

Then scan a QR code to watch the cuts on your phone and keep, change or drop each one.

When Opus Clip is the better pick

  • You want clips with zero editing and no AI agent of your own.
  • You work from a YouTube link rather than the file, or on a computer that can’t run a desktop app.
  • You want scheduling and posting built in.

gmclip is for people who want to control the cut. It’s version 0.1.0, and its installers aren’t signed yet.

Questions

Do I need an AI subscription?

Only for the agent features. Without one you can still mark moments yourself and do every edit by hand. With one, gmclip runs on your existing Claude, ChatGPT or other plan and adds no credits of its own.

Does my video get uploaded?

No. gmclip transcribes and renders on your computer. If you connect an agent, it reads the transcript and project through gmclip’s tools under your own account with its provider.

Can it make horizontal clips too?

Yes. Every cut keeps both 9:16 and 16:9, so the same edit becomes a Short and a YouTube video.
