What works the same

The core idea is Descript’s, and it’s a good one. gmclip transcribes a recording word by word and every word links to its frames. Delete a sentence and the video goes with it; restore it and it comes back. Trims snap to word edges, so a cut never lands halfway through a word. Tighten removes pauses and filler words in one step, and one undo brings them all back. Find and replace works across the transcript, and fixing a misheard word fixes it everywhere it appears in the captions.

Where gmclip is different

Built for Shorts and YouTube from the same recording

Every cut keeps a 9:16 and a 16:9 framing. Switch between them and the edit follows, with presets for full screen and screen-plus-facecam, a crop that follows the action, and blurred fill behind the picture. Captions burn in at 1–3 words with your keywords in yellow, and export runs at −16 LUFS so every clip has the same loudness.

Your agent, not a credit meter

gmclip has no AI of its own to sell. Its built-in MCP server lets the agent you already pay for (Claude Code, Codex, pi or opencode) use every feature: find the moments worth posting, draft a story, tighten a cut. Each edit shows live and can be undone, and nothing runs until you click.

Local by default

Your footage, transcripts and projects stay in folders you choose. There’s no upload step and no storage cap. Decoding and encoding use your operating system’s codecs, and the source is on GitHub for anyone to read or build.

When Descript is the better pick

  • You need to edit in a browser, on Linux, or with a team in the same project.
  • You want AI voice features such as overdub, or a studio-sound filter.
  • You record remote interviews and want recording and editing in one tool.
  • You need a signed, mature app today. gmclip is version 0.1.0, and its installers aren’t signed yet.

Other open-source options

Audapolis is a transcript-based editor focused on audio. auto-editor cuts by silence or motion from the command line rather than by transcript. Both are worth a look if you only need one of those jobs.

Questions

Is gmclip really free?

Yes. It’s open source under MIT or Apache-2.0, with no paid tier, no account and no watermark. If you use an AI agent with it, that agent runs on your own plan.

Does it need an internet connection?

Not to transcribe or edit. Transcription runs on your computer.

Can I open my Descript projects?

No. Export your original media from Descript and open that in gmclip; it transcribes it again on your machine.
