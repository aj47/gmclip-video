The app

gmclip runs on your computer. Transcription happens on your machine, and your footage, transcripts and projects stay in folders you choose. The app has no account and sends no usage data to us. When you connect your own AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, pi or opencode), that agent talks to its own provider under your account and its terms.

Which links bring people here

When a visit to gmclip.com starts, the page sends one count to our server with the page you landed on, the site that linked you (for example youtube.com), and any utm_ tags on the link. Clicks on a download, GitHub or “Get updates” link send one more count saying which link it was. That’s all: no IP address, device, browser fingerprint, visitor id or cookie is stored, so the counts can’t be tied to a person. The note of where your visit came from lives in your browser’s session storage and is gone when you close the tab.

To stop a runaway script from flooding the counts, the server keeps a one-way hash of your IP address and the current hour for at most a day. It isn’t stored with the counts.

Update emails

If you leave your email under “Get updates”, we store it with the date and use it only to tell you about new versions of gmclip. It’s stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. Email hi@techfren.net to have it removed.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Downloads come from GitHub Releases, which has its own privacy statement.
